Project Overview

Group 1's CEP repository serves as the central location for research, assignments, project documentation, and future deliverables associated with the CVRx project.

Our research currently examines topics related to sales technology, customer relationship management (CRM), CRM technology adoption, sales-force automation, healthcare sales, patient engagement, and sales performance.

This project hub will continue to expand as the team progresses through the Culminating Experience Project.
